Seqognito 🕶

version 0.1.0 testnet preview

A wallet for mixing. Your coins go into one transaction together with other people's and come out somewhere nobody can follow — and because Sequentia has confidential transactions, the amounts are hidden too: every output of the round, your change included, is a commitment rather than a number. It holds Bitcoin and Sequentia assets, receives and sends, and deliberately does nothing else.

Everything over Tor: the window has no network of its own, and the wallet will not send anything without a SOCKS proxy.
Two circuits, not one: your coins and your mixed addresses are registered over separate Tor circuits, so the coordinator sees two strangers.
Any node you like: point it at your own Sequentia and Bitcoin endpoints, .onion included.
Bitcoin too: parent-chain BTC is bridged in, mixed, and sent back out to an address you have never used.

Install · You need Tor running — a system tor daemon (port 9050) or Tor Browser (9150). Linux: make the .AppImage executable and run it. Windows: run the installer, or take the portable zip. On first start it asks which nodes to use, and encrypts your recovery phrase with a passphrase.

What it does and does not hide · The chain learns nothing about the amounts. The coordinator cannot link your coins to your mixed outputs, though it does see your amounts and your change. Your anonymity set is the round you were in — a round with two people in it hides you among two.

Pignus CLI

version 0.2.0 testnet preview

Collateralised lending from the command line, against your own node. Everything the lending site does — propose a loan, publish a funded offer, borrow, repay, liquidate — plus the one check the whole thing rests on: rebuild a vault’s address from the terms you agreed and refuse to sign if it differs.

What is inside

CLI, oracle and liquidator

pignus-cli · pignus-oracle · pignus-liquidator · pignusd
Source on GitHub
Verify before you sign: pignus-cli verify rebuilds the vault address from your terms and refuses if one atom of the debt, the strike or the oracle differs.
Run your own oracle: sign prices for your own markets and publish every attestation, so a liquidation can always be checked afterwards.
Run your own liquidator: liquidation is a permissionless race and anyone may enter it. Nothing about Pignus depends on the operator’s bot.

Pure Python, no build step.
